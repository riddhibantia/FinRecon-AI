# Operations Escalation Matrix
<!-- page: 1 -->
## High impact escalation
Under this synthetic escalation matrix, a discrepancy of INR 100000.00 or more, or evidence of repeated funds movement, requires the operations manager immediately. The analyst must attach source record identifiers and the unresolved difference. These are demonstration thresholds, not an automated severity rule in P3.

## Specialist ownership
Route missing batches, duplicate records and settlement delivery delays to payment operations. Route historical foreign exchange rate questions to treasury. Route disputed merchant terms to the contract owner. The reconciliation analyst retains case ownership until a human accepts the handoff.
<!-- page: 2 -->
## Unresolved case ageing
An unresolved case older than two calendar days must be reviewed by the operations manager. Record the last investigation action, missing evidence and next owner. Escalation does not imply financial loss or authorize a write-off. An AI draft must remain subject to human review.
