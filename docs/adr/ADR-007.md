# ADR-007 — Facts that must come from tools/database

- Status: Accepted (P8, enforced by tests).
- Context: The investigator must never invent transaction values,
  settlement status, fee rules, or policy requirements.
- Decision: Payment/ledger/settlement values come from case evidence
  and record tools; policy claims require retrieved excerpts with
  document citations verified against the source. The summary is
  grounded in cited values only.
- Consequences: Forged policy text or hostile case notes cannot add
  claims, change the draft action, or bypass human approval.
- Tests/artifacts: `ai-service/tests/test_agent.py`,
  `ai-service/tests/test_prompt_injection.py`,
  `ai-service/tests/test_api_boundaries.py`.
